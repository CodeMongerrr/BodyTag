# Refund policy

Source of truth: `apps/web/src/app/refund-policy/page.tsx` (live at `/refund-policy`). No change-of-mind refunds (business purchases). Takeover gives a closed ledger credit, refundable on request or after 30 idle days. Creator cancellation: brand chooses refund or equivalent content. Stake leftover refunded; cancellation treats GMV as zero. Non-waivable consumer rights preserved.
